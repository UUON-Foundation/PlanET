# PlanET

PlanET is a planned app for getting outside to clean up litter and to log the plants and wildlife you see.

**Status: not released.** Nothing in this project is available to use yet.

## What it will do
- Find litter clean-up spots near you.
- Let you log what you find: plants, wildlife, and litter.

## What is still being decided
- How people are rewarded for their work.
- How photos and location data are handled. A privacy notice comes before release.
- The name, and whether it is free to use.

## Built on
PlanET is part of the UUON Foundation (uuon.world).
